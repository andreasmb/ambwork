Pulse was one of the iPad’s first breakout apps: featured by Steve Jobs at WWDC 2010, named “App of the Week” in the iTunes Store, winner of Apple Design Awards, and acquired by LinkedIn in 2013. I designed the interface for its first version and the marketing for its launch, including two promotional films.
