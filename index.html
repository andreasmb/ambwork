Lever

Pulse Newsreader

Visual design, interface design, and promotional film for the popular iPad application Pulse for Alphonso Labs. The app was featured as the "App of the Week" in the iTunes Store and won the Apple Design Award.

With Akshay Kothari and Ankit Gupta. Pulse was acquired by LinkedIn in 2013.

Practice Fusion for iPad

Practice Fusion's electronic health record (EHR) system gives doctors fast access to their patients' information. The tablet format frees doctors from desktops and facilitates a face-to-face interaction between doctors and patients.

Winner of the IxDA award 2013 in the 'Optimizing' category. Interaction design, user research, prototyping. With Stefan Klocek, Jayson McCauliff, and Elisha Cook.

Bureau of Trade

An idiosyncratic fashion magazine and online store for men. The site curated items in groups that highlighted their shared aesthetics accompanied by witty commentary. Acquired by eBay in 2014.

UI/UX and front-end engineering. With Max Ellinger and Michael Moskowitz. Press

Chrome for a Cause

Worked with Google's marketing team to let the nascent Chrome community "donate" tabs to a charity of their choosing. The campaign contributed $1m to five worthy causes, with over 60m tabs opened in a five-day period.

With Sarah Nahm. Press
